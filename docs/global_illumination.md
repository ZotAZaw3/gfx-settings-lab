# Global Illumination (GI)

Covers: ambient / hemisphere fallback, irradiance light probes (spherical harmonics grid), screen-space GI (SSGI). Path-traced and ray-traced GI belong to the ray tracing thread.
Code: `src/modules/lighting/GIModule.cpp`, `shaders/lighting/probe_sh_project.comp`, `shaders/lighting/ssgi.comp`, `shaders/lighting/temporal_accum.comp`, SH evaluation in `shade.glsl`.

## Definition

Direct lighting covers light that goes source → surface → eye. Global illumination adds every extra bounce: sunlight hitting a red wall and tinting the white floor next to it (*color bleeding*), a room lit only by light through a window, the soft fill in shadowed areas. Without GI, shadows are pitch black or filled with a flat ambient color.

| Setting value | Technique |
|---|---|
| Off | constant ambient |
| Low | hemisphere ambient (sky color above, ground color below) + IBL diffuse |
| Medium | irradiance probe grid (L1 SH), trilinear interpolation |
| High | probe grid L2 SH + SSGI half res, 4 rays |
| Ultra | probe grid L2 SH + SSGI full res, 8 rays, temporal accumulation |

## How it works

### Irradiance probes (spherical harmonics)
1. Place probes on a 3D grid covering the level.
2. At each probe, render (or ray-cast) the surrounding radiance into a small cubemap.
3. Project that cubemap onto spherical harmonics: 9 coefficients per color channel for L2 (4 for L1). SH captures low-frequency lighting compactly.
4. At shading, find the 8 surrounding probes, trilinearly blend their SH, evaluate irradiance in direction `N`: `E(N) = Σ c_lm · Â_l · Y_lm(N)` with the cosine-lobe convolution `Â = {π, 2π/3, π/4}`.
5. Leaking through walls is the main problem: fix with visibility/depth per probe (DDGI-style Chebyshev test) or by weighting by `dot(N, dirToProbe)`.

### SSGI
Like SSAO, but when a sample ray hits the depth buffer, gather the **previous frame's lit color** at the hit instead of just counting the occlusion. Each pixel shoots a few cosine-distributed rays, ray-marches them through the depth buffer (same marcher as SSR), and accumulates `hitColor × albedo`. Temporal accumulation and a spatial denoiser turn a few noisy rays into a smooth result. Off-screen bounces fall back to the probe grid.

## Breakdown

| Stage | When | Output | Cost driver |
|---|---|---|---|
| Probe bake | load / on light change | SH coefficients (3D texture, 7×RGBA16F for L2) | probes × 6 face renders |
| Probe lookup | lighting shader | irradiance | 8 fetches × 7 textures |
| SSGI trace | per frame | noisy indirect | rays × steps × resolution |
| Temporal accumulate | per frame | history buffer | 1 reprojection |
| Spatial denoise | per frame | smooth indirect | taps |
| Apply | lighting | `diffuse_indirect = albedo × irradiance × AO` | free |

## Optimization

- **Bake probes** offline or incrementally (a few probes per frame), not every frame.
- **L1 SH** (4 coeffs) for low-end, L2 (9) for high.
- **Store SH in 3D textures** to get hardware trilinear interpolation for free.
- **SSGI at half or quarter res** with depth-aware upsampling. Indirect light is low frequency, so it tolerates this well.
- **Reuse the SSR marcher and HiZ** chain for SSGI rays.
- **Temporal accumulation** with variance clamping: 1–2 rays/pixel/frame is enough.
- **Combine with AO**: SSGI already includes near-field occlusion; with SSGI on, use AO only for probe/ambient term.

## Rebuild (from scratch)

1. Choose a grid (e.g. 8×4×8 over the scene bounds). For each probe position, render a 32² cubemap of the lit scene (or of albedo × direct light).
2. Project to SH: for each texel, direction `d`, solid angle `dω`, accumulate `c_lm += L(d) · Y_lm(d) · dω`.
3. Upload into 3D textures (L1: 3 RGBA16F textures, one per channel with 4 coeffs; L2: 7 RGBA16F packed).
4. Lighting shader: `uvw = (worldPos − gridMin) / gridSize`, sample SH, evaluate with the cosine kernel, clamp ≥ 0.
5. SSGI: fullscreen pass; per pixel, N cosine-weighted directions around normal, march in screen space (8–16 steps), on hit read `prevFrameColor`, weight by `dot(N, dir)`; average.
6. Temporal: reproject with velocity buffer, `history = mix(history, current, 0.1)`, clamp to neighborhood min/max.
7. Composite: `indirect = albedo * (ssgi.a > 0 ? mix(probe, ssgi.rgb, ssgi.a) : probe) * ao`.
8. Settings: `giQuality` → technique, probe SH order, SSGI rays & resolution.
